# Commercial terms for the agency agreement (pilot, 2026-10-17)

> The service agreement itself (BR-62: one agreement with the data processing annex, signed by the agency manager) is drafted by the lawyer and is not in this repo. This page lists the commercial terms it should carry, so the agreement, the quotes (`sales/quote/`) and the pricing pages (`sales/pricing-page/`) say the same thing. Numbers come from `PRICING` in `sales/quote/quote.src.html`. Terms marked (pilot) are tested with the first agencies and reviewed after the pilot.

## Package and counting
- The agency buys a yearly traveler package: 100, 250, 500, 1,000 or 2,000 travelers (other amounts by special agreement).
- A traveler is counted once per trip, when the trip is first sent to them. Drafts and trips cancelled before sending are not counted. Groups have no separate package.
- All agency users (agents and managers) are included. No user limit (decision 2026-10-16; to revisit).

## Payment options
1. **Annual commitment, paid monthly**: the base price. The whole package is available from day one.
2. **Annual prepaid**: 10% off the annual price, one payment.
3. **Monthly without commitment** (pilot): 15% above the base price. Cancel with 30 days notice. The package accrues month by month (1/12 per month).
- Prices are in ₪ and exclude VAT. A quote is valid for 30 days.

## When the package runs out
- Keep working without interruption. Either buy extra travelers at the package's extra-traveler price (₪22.5 / 18.5 / 15.5 / 12.5 / 10.5 for 100 / 250 / 500 / 1,000 / 2,000), or move to the next package and pay only the difference until the end of the year.
- The agency manager gets an alert when 20% of the package is left.

## Unused travelers (pilot)
- On renewal, up to 20% of the package's unused travelers carry over to the next year. They expire at the end of that year. Nothing carries over without renewal, and there are no refunds for unused travelers.

## Launch offer (draft, if approved)
- First 50 travelers free within 90 days, no credit card. Founders price: 20% off the first year for the first 30 agencies until 31.12.2026. Referral: 100 extra travelers for each of the two agencies. See `docs/sales/launch-offer.md`.
