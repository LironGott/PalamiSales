# Launch offer (recommendation, 2026-10-16, not approved)

Besides the design partner. Goal: agencies try PALAMI with real travelers, and the name spreads through travelers and agency word of mouth.

## Recommendation: three layers
1. **"50 הנוסעים הראשונים עלינו"** (usage trial, not time). Free for the first 50 travelers (≈ 20 trips), up to 90 days, no credit card. Why: agencies publish trips weeks before departure, so a free month may end before the value is felt; travelers are our value metric. Cost to us: about ₪60 per agency in usage + 2–3 hours of onboarding.
2. **"מחיר מייסדים"** for the first 30 agencies that join until a date: 20% off the first year, locked. Why: urgency and scarcity without lowering the public price anchor. No permanent discount and no free plan (support costs per agency).
3. **"סוכנות מביאה סוכנות"**: both agencies get 100 extra travelers (≈ ₪120 cost each). The Israeli agency community is small and talks; referrals are the cheapest channel.

Plus free exposure: "מופעל על ידי PALAMI" in every traveler app, and asking happy agencies for a quote or logo for the site.

## Avoid
- A broad discount (sets a low anchor that is hard to raise).
- A free month (low-frequency use; value is felt only when travelers use the app).
- A permanent free plan (support cost per agency, attracts agencies that will not pay).

## Measure
Activation: first trip sent within 7 days. Usage: travelers sent in the trial. Conversion: paid within 14 days after the 50 travelers or 90 days. Referral count.
