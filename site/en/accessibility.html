Accessibility statement

Last updated: 15 October 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website easily, including people with disabilities. We have worked to make it accessible and will keep improving it.

Accessibility level

The website was built to meet Israeli Standard IS 5568 and the WCAG 2.2 guidelines at level AA.

What we did

  • The whole site can be used with a keyboard, with a clear focus indicator and a “Skip to content” link.
  • Headings and landmarks that screen readers understand, and alternative text for informative images.
  • Sufficient color contrast, and information is never conveyed by color alone.
  • The site adapts to mobile, and text can be enlarged up to 200% without losing content.
  • The animation has a pause button and the site respects the device’s “reduce motion” setting.
  • Every form field has a label, and error messages are announced and explain what to fix.

Known limitations

Screenshots of the system are images in Hebrew, with a text description of what they show. If anything on the site is not accessible to you, we’d like to hear about it and fix it.

How we tested

The site was tested with an automated checker (axe) and manual keyboard navigation, in Chrome on desktop and at mobile screen size.
